# diamonds.csv

The diamond price dataset that ships with ggplot2 and seaborn: 53,940 stones
with carat, cut, colour, clarity, dimensions and price.

**This is the real data.** Mean price 3932.80, mean carat 0.7979, 53,940 rows.

Until 0.9.0 this file held a simulated dataset with the same shape and
different numbers: a mean price of 2361.73 against the true 3932.80. See
`docs/DATASETS.md`.
