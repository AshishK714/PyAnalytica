# tips.csv

The restaurant tipping dataset that ships with seaborn: 244 meals recorded by
one waiter, with the bill, the tip, and who was at the table.

**This is the real data.** Mean bill 19.7859, mean tip 2.9983, 244 rows -- the
figures anyone comparing against seaborn or a textbook will see.

Until 0.9.0 this file held a simulated dataset with the same shape and
different numbers: a mean tip of 3.98 against the true 3.00. See
`docs/DATASETS.md`.
